# 🇺🇸 English

## 📌 About the Project

**F1 Collection** is a web platform developed to organize, preserve and present a Formula 1 collection through a digital experience.

The project transforms collection content into a structured web environment, making it easier to explore Formula 1 items and historical material through an organized and accessible interface.

The platform was developed with a strong focus on the visual presentation of the collection, navigation and preservation of its identity.

---

## 🏁 The Collection

The collection presented on this website belongs to **Mauro Matta**.

The purpose of the platform is to provide a digital environment where the collection can be organized and presented while preserving the identity and history behind the original archive.

This project separates two important responsibilities:

- **Collection & Content:** Mauro Matta
- **Web Development:** Camilo Tozzi

---

## ✨ Main Features

- Digital presentation of a Formula 1 collection
- Organization of historical collection content
- Visual navigation through collection items
- Formula 1 themed interface
- Structured presentation of the archive
- Responsive web experience
- Online access to the collection
- Deployment through Vercel
